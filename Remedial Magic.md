# Remedial Magic

**Remedial Magic** is a rules-light tabletop RPG of summer school, questionable ingredients, and surprisingly durable students.

Welcome to **Pengorgon's Remedial Institute for Summer Magical Studies** or **PRISMS**. Your last magical mishap earned you a summer of supervised instruction. Fortunately, Professor Quill is supervising through his eyelids. While he dozes over your worksheets, you and your classmates slip out to chase rumors, help friends, and investigate things the school insists are perfectly ordinary. Get back before he wakes. Preferably with the same number of eyebrows.

Each student casts from **one pouch of colored six-sided dice**. Draw and roll: matching faces decide whether the spell succeeds; the most common color decides what kind of magic manifests. You wanted a Fire Sword. Your ingredients supplied **Fires Word**. The target is now being attacked by a swarm of romance novels.

- **Build your own troublesome pouch.** Choose a magical specialty, earn ingredients, and refine your mix across five levels. Compulsory practical supplies keep even accomplished students guessing.
- **Help invent the surprises.** Casters choose their unexpected spells and backfires. Wild magic brings a useful effect and a complication; classmates offer ideas when inspiration fails, and the Professor settles the result.
- **Bring unfinished business.** A simple background knack connects you to people, promises, and problems the school has unwisely allowed onto campus.
- **Keep the action moving.** Play through description rather than measured ranges. Students are hearty, recovery is forgiving, and missing eyebrows are an educational outcome.

One player serves as the **Professor**; the others play the students. Bring colored D6s, an opaque pouch for each student, and a willingness to explain yourself. Expect teamwork, magical improvisation, narrow escapes from suspicious faculty, and small errands that become rather large secrets. Your adventures may eventually count as practical credit. Leaving class still requires an explanation.

## Concerning practical supervision

Your **Professor (DM)** describes the situation, confirms difficulty, and checks that proposed results follow the regulations. The caster normally chooses their own unexpected spells and mishaps. When you ask for help, classmates offer ideas and the Professor chooses one; an obvious opportunity already present in the scene may also supply the explanation. See [[Spellcasting#Explaining an unintended result|Explaining an unintended result]]. You and your classmates describe your actions and make the required draws. The numerical instructions are the Institute's practical record system; do not improvise those along with the spells.

The Professor is the player running the game; Professor Quill is the teacher currently asleep in it. Quill's absence does not prevent declaring spells or agreeing difficulty at the table.

This handbook contains the current regulations. It contains no promise that the greenhouse has read them.
