
# The Summer Curriculum

Consult: [[Spellcasting]] | [[Dice and Mana]] | [[PRISMS]]

Nominate the school in which you show the most promise. This is your **specialty**. It does not excuse attendance in other subjects or prohibit you from attempting their techniques.

The Institute teaches eight schools. Your department issues four starting dice in its favored color. Other mana may be used in its exercises; Evocation staff are particularly accustomed to this. Consult [[Students and Advancement|Admissions and Progress]] for your allowance and specialty draw.

## On technique and substance

**School describes the technique. Mana describes the substance and expression.** An evocator can attack with Red flame, Blue water, or Purple words. An abjurer can protect with White wards, Brown stone, or Green hedges.

Some combinations are more natural than others. Necromancy often uses Black, but that does not make every Black spell Necromancy: directing a ghostly blast can be Evocation. Illusion creates false sensory impressions; Enchantment changes the subject's actual attention or feelings.

Declare your technique and agree its school with your Professor before drawing. An unexpected mana result does not change that school or your draw. An exercise suitable for two departments is recorded under one; collecting both specialty allowances is not interdisciplinary study.
