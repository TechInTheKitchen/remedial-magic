## Previous experience and outstanding business

On admission, record **one background knack**: something you learned before arriving at Prisms. Choose a modest area of ordinary experience, such as Greenhouse Assistant, Kitchen Regular, Chronic Sneak, or Former Junior Athlete. Your magical specialty remains a separate entry.

Finish this sentence: **"I know my way around ___, and ___ still needs something from me."** The second blank names a person, responsibility, or unfinished problem that can follow you into summer instruction. Agree the connection with your Professor. It should offer something you want to pursue, not merely another reason to receive detention.

| Background knack      | Outstanding business                                                                                                |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Greenhouse Assistant  | Groundskeeper Moss trusted you with a seedling that now requests Romance Novels on the daily.                       |
| Kitchen Regular       | The cook will overlook the biscuit incident if you recover a recipe stolen by a pudding.                            |
| Chronic Sneak         | You promised a younger student to retrieve their confiscated keepsake; the cupboard has changed corridors, again.   |
| Former Junior Athlete | Your old teammate needs help before the inter-school match. Their lucky boots have been taken by the opposing side. |
| Family Shop Assistant | A parcel addressed to your family arrived at Prisms. Something inside keeps signing for further deliveries.         |

**A knack establishes ordinary competence, not a bonus.** Recognize familiar tools, know everyday customs, and handle safe tasks within your experience without a roll. You might identify a thirsty plant or know which kitchen door the cook uses. It does not automatically reveal concealed magical causes or resolve a dangerous obstacle. Consequential or uncertain work still follows the normal casting procedure: no extra dice, separate skill checks, or changes to your specialty draw. Practical assignments still require magical application.

The Professor brings outstanding business into assignments through a familiar face, a useful clue, or a small additional objective. A connection may supply access or context, but it does not replace the assignment's challenges or grant another spell effect. Give every student a chance to matter; nobody's prior employment appoints them leader of the cohort.

At debrief, record how your connection changed. A fulfilled promise can lead to a new request, a friendship, or an explanation you would rather not give. Choose any new outstanding business together; it grants no additional advancement or supplies.

## Collecting your first allowance

1. Nominate your specialty in [[Schools of Magic|The Summer Curriculum]].
2. Collect **four dice of your department's favored color**.
3. Request **four dice of one different color of your choice**.
4. Accept **four randomly allocated colors**, one at a time. Repeats are allowed, including your selected colors. Reroll any allocation that would leave more than six of one color in your twelve-die pouch.

Record both selected colors. These are your default preferences when the Supply Office prepares compulsory practical supplements.

Random initial colors are allocated with a D8; reroll 8:

| Result | Color |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Red |
| 2 | Blue |
| 3 | Green |
| 4 | Brown |
| 5 | White |
| 6 | Purple |
| 7 | Black |

Your allowance reflects departmental aptitude, personal interests, and whatever was on the shelf when the cupboard unlocked itself.

## Pengorgon's Balanced Reagent Policy

> [!quote] Supply cupboard notice
> No single ingredient may occupy more than half a student's component allowance. The Institute has already replaced the roof associated with that experiment.

**No color may exceed half your pouch.** Round the permitted count down for an odd total. This applies to the complete working pouch, including loaned practical supplies, and to your permanent allowance after those loans are returned.

The restriction governs preparation, not a random draw. Drawing mostly or entirely one color is permitted. Departmental preference does not exempt you from the policy.

Use **one opaque pouch and one combined draw pool**. Permanent and loaned ingredients work identically. Record loans on paper, then mix them into that pouch. A second bag labeled "ingredients I would rather not use" will be emptied into the first.

## Summer standing

Your cohort advances together after each major practical assignment. Four assignments take you from Level 1 to Level 5. Delivering a letter or retrieving a spoon does not count, however dramatic your account of the journey.

An unauthorized expedition can count as a major practical once its learning is established at debrief. Escaping afternoon study is not itself worth a level; dealing with the substantial magical problem you found outside may be. Academic progress and consequences for leaving class are handled separately.

Use your specialty draw when the declared technique belongs to your department, regardless of which mana appears. Use the ordinary draw for other schools. Greater standing does not change matching requirements; difficult work does not authorize a larger handful.

Each advancement permits two additional permanent supplies in chosen colors. Levels 3 and 5 also authorize a larger specialty draw. Harder assignments introduce larger compulsory packets. Familiarity with your own cupboard is not a complete education.

If an authorized draw exceeds your pouch, draw the whole pouch once. Never return dice midway through a cast to draw them again.

## Compulsory practical supplements

Collect the packet listed for your current level before the first consequential cast of every substantial expedition or pressured practical, including Level 5 work. One packet serves the whole assignment; the Office does not issue another each time something bites. Between assignments, use your permanent supplies.

These packets are issued for afternoon study before Quill settles into his chair. Take the issued supplies with you if you slip out. Their compulsory use does not mean the staff approved your destination. An expedition begun unexpectedly still uses the same packet allowance and allocation procedure before its first consequential cast; no cupboard theft or additional supply check is required.

### Allocation and use

1. Declare one or two **preferred colors for your present studies**, defaulting to your initial selections. This declaration lasts for the assignment and does not recolor supplies you own.
2. Allocate packet dice randomly from colors **outside those preferences**. One preferred color leaves six eligible colors; two leave five.
3. Determine the final combined pouch size before allocation. Add dice one at a time, rejecting a color if it would exceed half that final size. Select uniformly from the remaining legal colors; repeats are allowed.
4. Record the packet's color counts and mix every addition into your existing pouch.

To select uniformly from the eligible colors, number the current list, roll a D6, and reroll unused results. Update the eligible list whenever a color reaches its permitted count.

Packet dice are compulsory. Return them after every cast along with all other dice. Do not discard, exchange, transfer, or recolor supplies between casts. Color changes are authorized only through practical rewards and Level 5 debriefs. Only one assignment packet may be active at a time.

The packet changes your mana balance without changing your authorized draw. An unfamiliar ingredient may save the day. You are expected to notice when it does.

### Returning supplies at debrief

For an assignment begun at Levels 1–4:

1. Advance one level, up to Level 5.
2. Keep **two dice as permanent rewards**, choosing their colors from all seven. Both may be the same color if the final permanent allowance respects the half-pouch limit.
3. Return the surplus. For accurate counting, remove the packet's entire original contribution using its recorded counts, then add the two chosen-color rewards.
4. Record your permanent composition and confirm that no color exceeds half its total.

Counters of the same color are interchangeable. You need not identify the particular die responsible for the incident.

You retain two portions you have learned to prepare yourself; the school reclaims the surplus. An assignment can conclude after a spectacular mishap or faculty intervention. A clean success is not required. Pending supplies remain compulsory until debrief.

If an assignment cannot be resumed, your Professor closes it with a debrief, applies an appropriate faculty consequence, and grants its normal advancement and color choices. The Office awards learning, not pristine upholstery.

### Worked supply record

An incoming evocator receives four Red, requests four Blue, and randomly receives two Red, one Green, and one Purple: **six Red, four Blue, one Green, one Purple**. Red is at the permitted limit.

Their first packet contains two Green, one Brown, one White, one Purple, and one Black. All eighteen dice enter the same pouch. Evocation still draws seven.

At debrief, remove that six-die packet and add two chosen-color rewards. The new fourteen-die permanent allowance permits at most seven of one color. One reward can be Red, but the other must differ. Choosing Blue yields **seven Red, five Blue, one Green, one Purple**. Both rewards could instead be Blue, or one Blue and one White.

## Level 5 and graduation

Level 5 is the highest summer standing. The final practical adds fourteen compulsory dice to your twenty permanent supplies: one pouch of thirty-four. At debrief return the whole packet. Graduation recognizes the outcome; it does not award another level or permanent pair.

Further Level 5 expeditions also require a fourteen-die packet. Their debrief may authorize recoloring two existing permanent dice, within the half-pouch limit, rather than increasing capacity.
