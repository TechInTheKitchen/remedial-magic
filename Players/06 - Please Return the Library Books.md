## Assignment Two: Please Return the Library Books

An afternoon expedition for **Level 2 students**, following [[Players/05 - Please Water the Greenhouse|Please Water the Greenhouse]]. This counts as your second major practical when you return for debrief.

> [!quote] Note folded inside a library receipt
> My book has come back without me. Now the returns desk says I'm overdue as well. I'm hiding in the reading room. Please hurry before it gets me.
>
> ~ Nell

### Instructions to students

Quill has asked you to write about what you learned in the greenhouse. He has reached the third sentence of his own instructions and fallen asleep. A paper receipt crawls across your desk, unfolds, and presents the note above. 

Nell is a classmate who lent you a book, covered for your absence, or helped with someone's unfinished business. Agree on the connection together. She was returning a borrowed book during her permitted library visit. Something has gone wrong.

You have **14 permanent dice** each. Mix in today's **eight-die practical packet**, drawn from the shared component pouch using your declared preferred colors and the usual supply rules. That gives you **22 dice in one pouch** before any specimens or corrective supplies. Your ordinary draw is **five** and your specialty draw is **eight**. Begin at **10 Composure**.

Find Nell, work out why the library is recalling things it never lent, and stop the collection before it reaches the rest of the school. The librarian is supervising examinations in another wing. You can fetch staff if you need help, though you will have to explain why you left class.

You hear the library's warning bell through the open window. A moment later, your chair slides an inch toward the door.

At debrief, advance to **Level 3**. Return all temporary supplies, then add **two permanent dice in colors you choose**. Your permanent allowance becomes **16 dice**, with no more than eight of one color. Your specialty draw increases to **nine** for future casts; your ordinary draw stays five.
