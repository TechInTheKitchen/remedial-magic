
# About the Institute

**Pengorgon's Remedial Institute for Summer Magical Studies**

You may call us **Prisms** in conversation. Use the full name on official correspondence, particularly correspondence concerning repair costs.

Consult: [[Remedial Magic]] | [[Dice and Mana]]

> [!quote] Official motto
> A brighter future through adequate supervision.

We provide summer instruction in magical theory, component handling, and judgment. You will catch up with your classmates and demonstrate that last term was an isolated occurrence. Saving the school is commendable. It does not automatically excuse how the school came to require saving.

Our name reflects the many colors of mana brought together under one roof. Please help keep that roof attached.

## Supervised independent study

Professor Quill oversees your afternoon study period from a comfortable chair. His spectacles remain pointed at the class even when his eyes do not. He calls this "reflective observation." Students call it the best opportunity to leave.

Most adventures begin with a whispered request, an intriguing noise, or unfinished business that cannot wait until the timetable permits it. Slip out, investigate, and try to return before Quill finishes resting his eyes. Corridors, prefects, borrowed keys, and evidence of your absence are part of the trouble. Quill sometimes wakes at precisely the wrong moment; no separate sleep roll or countdown is required.

The Institute recognizes learning demonstrated during an unauthorized expedition. Practical credit and permission to leave class are recorded separately. Staff are protective, occasionally useful, and often inconvenient; being caught leads to comic consequences and further adventures rather than the end of your education.
