
# About the Institute

**Pengorgon's Remedial Institute for Summer Magical Studies**

You may call us **Prisms** in conversation. Use the full name on official correspondence, particularly correspondence concerning repair costs.

Consult: [[Remedial Magic]] | [[Dice and Mana]]

> [!quote] Official motto
> A brighter future through adequate supervision.

We provide summer instruction in magical theory, component handling, and judgment. You will catch up with your classmates and demonstrate that last term was an isolated occurrence. If you save the school, please be prepared to explain what endangered it.

Our name reflects the many colors of mana brought together under one roof. Please help keep that roof intact.

## Supervised independent study

Professor Quill oversees your afternoon study period from a comfortable chair. His spectacles remain pointed at the class even when his eyes do not. He calls this "reflective observation." Students call it the best opportunity to leave.

A whispered request, a strange noise, or a promise to a friend can be enough to get you out of your seat. Slip out, investigate, and try to return before Quill finishes resting his eyes. Corridors, prefects, borrowed keys, and evidence of your absence are part of the trouble. 

The Institute may give you credit for what you learned while sneaking out. It may also give you detention for sneaking out. Staff will keep you safe, though you may end up finishing your adventure under supervision.

## Faculty instructions for an afternoon expedition

The Professor at the table prepares one immediate reason to leave class, a problem worth investigating, and a reason to come back. Use a student's unfinished business when it fits. Give the group a reason to care about the problem before they risk another detention.

Tell students what they can plainly see. Give them more than one way to find an essential clue. If an investigation fails, add trouble while leaving a way to keep looking. Set difficulty from the declared effect, and let different schools and mana supply different solutions.

Before the first cast that matters, issue the current practical packet and begin shared Suspicion at zero. During the expedition, offer meaningful discoveries and announce impending trouble. Quill's waking and prefects' inquiries follow the circumstances and [[Threats and Faculty|the attention register]], without additional sleep checks or an unseen countdown.

Close the expedition when its central problem is settled, safely abandoned, or handed over with an account of what happened. Ask what the students learned, return their temporary supplies, and award advancement if the expedition counts as a major practical. An unsuccessful major practical may still earn advancement; a short personal errand does not automatically award a level. A student can learn a great deal and still owe the groundskeeper an apology.
